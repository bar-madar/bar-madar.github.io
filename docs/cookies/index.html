Cookie policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This website does not use cookies, local storage, session storage, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or any similar technology. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Your browser may keep its own cache of the pages and images to load them faster. That is a normal browser feature, and you control it in your browser's settings.

Links to other sites, such as GitHub and Agensi, take you to sites with their own cookie policies.

The Tokenmeter dashboard runs on your own computer and doesn't use cookies either.
