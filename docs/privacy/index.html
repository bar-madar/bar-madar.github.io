Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy covers this website (bar-madar.github.io), the Tokenmeter plugin and dashboard, and the TokenDiet skill. They are made by Bar Madar, an independent developer, who is responsible for them. The short version: none of them collect personal data.

This website

  • It uses no cookies, local storage, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • It has no forms and asks you for no information.
  • It loads no fonts, scripts or images from other sites.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Tokenmeter and TokenDiet

  • Both run entirely on your computer. They read files there (Claude Code's session logs, and your agent instruction files for TokenDiet) and send nothing anywhere.
  • Tokenmeter writes one file, tokenmeter-live.json, in your Claude config folder. TokenDiet writes backups inside your project and a short audit summary (numbers and finding titles, no file contents) in ~/.claude/tokendiet/. These files stay on your computer.
  • Neither tool contains analytics, telemetry or update checks.

Buying TokenDiet

TokenDiet is sold through Agensi, which acts as the seller and processes payment through Stripe. Your purchase data is handled under Agensi's terms and privacy policy. If Agensi shares order details with me to resolve a support request, I use them only for that purpose.

Support requests

Support runs through GitHub issues, which are public and governed by GitHub's terms and privacy statement. Please don't post personal details there. If a request needs private information, say so in the issue and I'll suggest a private channel.

Your rights

Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law and other laws that may apply to you, such as the GDPR, you can ask what personal data is held about you and have it corrected or deleted. Since neither the site nor the tools collect personal data, there is normally nothing to provide. For any privacy question, open an issue.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
